# Deepcast — Project Brief

*Written 24 July 2026, the day the watch page redesign shipped. For strategic advisors: decisions and reasoning, not implementation. Supersedes the 22 July brief.*

## What Deepcast is

Deepcast optimizes for depth over reach. It is an invite-only network where substantive films spread through trusted relationships — no feed, no algorithm, no public link. A film travels only because one person chose one other person and handed them a ticket; trust built person to person carries it through trusted networks toward deep impact and resonance. Deepcasting, not broadcasting. Depth is the heart of the platform — the tickets, the constellation, and invite-only itself all exist to serve it. Founded and built by Ien Chi, a filmmaker who helped grow a YouTube channel to five million subscribers and a billion views, and watched platform incentives bend everything toward clickbait; Deepcast is the counter-bet.

## Standing product laws (unchanged, do not re-derive)

- **Shares are custom unique links, not emails.** The sender types only a first name and gets a bare link to deliver personally — the delivery is the human moment; no pre-written message, ever. The old email-invite system is retired (links already sent keep working forever).
- **Every ticket has a permanent number.** Per film, issued in link-creation order, immutable: a deleted ticket leaves a gap forever, and nothing ever renumbers. The filmmaker always holds №1 on their own film; invitees start at №2.
- **Claiming signs you in.** A new claimant becomes a full signed-in user instantly. Security boundary held deliberately: instant sign-in happens only when the claim just created the account — typing an email can never open an account that already exists.
- **One claim per person per film.** A duplicate claim voids the link, returns the sender's ticket, and says, verbatim: *"You already hold this film."* The sender's list keeps the row as history: *"Already held this film — ticket returned."* People are people, not rows.
- **One definition of "who exists."** Every surface reads a single shared rule for which tickets are real. Voided tickets count nowhere; seeded demo ghosts appear only per each film's show_ghosts rule and are never numbered. New surfaces must read the shared rule, never invent their own.
- **The journey sentence counts your whole downstream.** *"This film has reached [X] people. [Y] of them through you."* — Y includes shares to any depth. Numerals always.
- **Copy is founder-approved verbatim or it doesn't ship.** First names only, everywhere; an email address is never displayed as a person's name; the first-name box refuses emails with *"Just their first name — no email needed."*

## Decisions and reasoning worth keeping from this week

- **Sparse-and-honest won.** Early films show single-digit counts against a 100 goal with no milestones row. Accepted deliberately: absence reads as nothing, and the row's first appearance at 100 is itself a quiet earned event.
- **The emblem's sparse state is a known design gap.** The mock composed a 47-deep chain; real chains of 1–2 read empty. Interim translate-centering shipped; the true sparse composition (chains of 1–3, the first concentric circles of a film's spread) is an open design task with the geometry documented.
- **Process held under pressure.** The agent once committed a fix directly to main — caught by the push gate, reprimanded, redone on a branch. A suspected regression (unpinned dashboard sidebar) was proven by rendering both branches to have never existed as a feature at all — now a properly scoped parked task with a pre-written test defining done. "Never touch main," "diagnose before fix," and "narration is not proof" all earned their keep.
- **The rail counts generosity, not consumption (25 July).** The watch page's big number switched from viewers-who-claimed to tickets shared — the act the page exists to inspire is the act it now measures. The milestone ladder didn't move; 100 stays the first rung. Same day, the founder ratified the standing vocabulary: "ticket" is the noun, "shared" is the verb, "Pass it on" belongs to the button alone — every viewer surface now says "shared," never "given." The invitation letter was also revised: the private-invitation stamp now opens it, the headline reads "shared a ticket with you," and the single-use line came off the page entirely.

## How the work runs

Ien (non-technical) directs; an AI coding agent builds. The rules that keep that safe, battle-tested again this week: the agent commits but never pushes — Ien pushes, and production deploys from that; no production change of any kind without Ien's explicit approval in that session (a permissions prompt is not approval); diagnose before fixing, report-only first; one logical change per commit, full suite before it lands; Ien verifies with his own eyes before any merge — narration, screenshots, and green checkmarks are evidence, not proof. All films are test films now (founder ruling, 24 July), but The New Narrative remains the designated test-claim film — and a local run writes to the real production database, so a "test" claim still mints a real, permanent ticket number. Durable rules live in code and documents, never in an agent's promise to remember: CLAUDE.md (implementation layer) and this brief (strategy layer) are both reconciled against shipped reality at the end of every working day.
