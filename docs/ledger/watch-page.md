- **The story's expand control (founder direction 2026-09-16; design-gate decisions 2026-09-17, branch `viewer-polish`; spec §3d):** desktop ≥900px shows the epigraph and the FIRST paragraph only, that paragraph fading out toward its end (a MASK — `STORY_FADE_MASK` in `src/lib/storyExpand.js`, alpha only, the real background shows through, no colour hard-coded), then `Read the rest` + a 10px chevron in the in-place-action caps style; pressing it reveals the rest, removes the fade and the control — no re-collapse. **The fade REVERSES the 16 September "no fade mask" line — the founder's call on the 17th.** The hidden paragraphs are display:none while collapsed (out of the accessibility tree and tab order).
